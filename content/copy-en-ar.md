# Valley Agriculture — Site Copy (English / العربية)

The single source of truth for site text in both languages. It is used to seed the CMS and `messages/en.json` / `messages/ar.json`.

**Arabic style:** Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى المعاصرة), formal but warm. It is adapted for meaning, not translated word for word. Western digits (0–9) are used for specs and phone numbers. Copy marked *(old site)* is carried over from valleyagrotrading.com and adapted.

**Translation notes**
- **Company name:** the logo stays in Latin script ("VALLEY"). In Arabic running text the brand is **فالي**, and the legal name is **فالي لتجارة الجرارات والمعدات الزراعية ذ.م.م** (W.L.L = ذات مسؤولية محدودة ← ذ.م.م).
- **Gender:** direct address uses the masculine singular (تبحث، أخبرنا), which is the standard generic form in Arabic marketing copy. Form labels and the WhatsApp message are written to be gender-neutral.
- **Terms:** tractor = جرار (pl. جرارات); model = طراز; quote = عرض سعر; after-sales = خدمات ما بعد البيع; bale = بالة (pl. بالات); centre pivot irrigation = الري المحوري.
- **Review:** have a native Arabic speaker on the client side approve this copy before launch. Pay particular attention to the hero headline and the technical terms.
